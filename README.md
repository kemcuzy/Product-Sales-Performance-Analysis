# Product Sales & Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyses 200,000 retail sales records from 2023–2024 to understand revenue growth, profitability, product category performance, geographical sales, and customer contribution.

The objective is to transform transactional sales data into actionable business insights that support evidence-based decision-making.

## Business questions

* How did revenue and profit change between 2023 and 2024?
* Which categories contribute most to revenue and profit?
* Which regions and states perform best?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* What can customer-level analysis reveal about retention opportunities?
* How can the findings inform business strategy?

## Tools and methods

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot tables and summary tables
* KPI analysis
* Comparative and exploratory analysis
* Dashboard planning and business reporting

## Dataset

The primary worksheet contains 200,000 records and 14 columns, including order date, customer name, location, product category, product name, quantity, unit price, revenue, and profit.

## Key findings

1. Revenue increased modestly between 2023 and 2024.
2. Electronics generated the highest category revenue.
3. Home & Furniture generated the highest total category profit.
4. The East was the strongest region by revenue.
5. Tempur-Pedic Mattress led the product revenue summary.
6. Category profit margins varied considerably.

## Recommendations

* Investigate category-level profitability and pricing.
* Monitor regional revenue, demand, and customer counts.
* Improve inventory planning using product-level demand.
* Validate customer identifiers before measuring loyalty.
* Introduce routine KPI and data-quality monitoring.



## Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates how sales records can be transformed into a business-focused analytical story. It highlights the importance of assessing revenue alongside profit, product demand, geographical performance, and customer behaviour.
